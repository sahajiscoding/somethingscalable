Architecture & Pipeline

How Moonshot AI Kimi-K3 Evaluates Exams

Split-pipeline execution prevents timeouts while maintaining high grading fidelity.

Step 01

Document Ingestion

Accepts PDFs, scans, photos, and digital text. Digital pages extract text; scanned scripts render to 150 DPI images.

Step 02

Vision OCR Extraction

Kimi-K3 transcribes questions, expected answers, and student handwriting in parallel single-request batches.

Step 03

Alignment & Scoring

Pure code aligns question numbers; Kimi-K3 marks each item against the rubric, enforcing step marks and penalties.

Step 04

Report & Analytics

Generates real-time scoreboard, deduction tags, granular student feedback, and downloadable JSON & Markdown.

Scoring Reference

Rubric Studio & Penalty Architecture

Understand how Kimi-K3 applies step marks, keyword checkpoints, and optional penalty criteria.

Criteria 01

Bad Handwriting

Applied when handwriting is cramped, erratic, or difficult to parse, slowing evaluation.

Deduction range: −0.5 to −2.0 marks.
OCR Noise Guarantee: Recognition artefacts are never penalized against the student.
Criteria 02

Flawed / Bad Diagrams

Applies to ray optics, circuit diagrams, geometric proofs, and free-body figures lacking required labels or arrows.

Deduction range: −1.0 to −3.0 marks.
Rule: Diagram must match the rubric's required components and annotations.
Criteria 03

Unreadable / Smudged Data

Applies when critical intermediate data (e.g. numerical constants, signs, indices) are smudged or scribbled over.

Deduction range: −1.0 to −2.0 marks.
Rule: Only penalizes if ambiguity obscures the student's mathematical logic.
Criteria 04

Poor Formatting / Missing Steps

Applies when a student skips mandatory working steps or presents disconnected equations.

Deduction range: −0.5 to −1.5 marks.
Rule: Step marks awarded proportionally according to the official marking scheme.

Sample Rubric & Step Mark Breakdown

How a 5-mark question is evaluated across step schemes.

Q1: Solve x² − 3x − 10 = 0 (5 Marks) Standard Step Breakdown
Step 1 (Formula Identification, 1.5 marks): Statement of quadratic formula x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac)) / 2a.
Step 2 (Discriminant Evaluation, 1.5 marks): Correct calculation of Δ = (-3)² - 4(1)(-10) = 49.
Step 3 (Square Root & Substitution, 1.0 mark): √49 = 7, yielding x = (3 ± 7) / 2.
Step 4 (Final Roots, 1.0 mark): Both roots explicitly stated: x = 5 and x = -2.

MID-TERM SCIENCE EXAM - Class X
Time: 2 hours

1. Solve the quadratic equation x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0. Show all working and state both roots. (5 marks)

2. State Lenz's law and draw a labelled diagram of a bar magnet falling through a copper tube, showing the direction of the induced current. (6 marks)

3. On what date was the Treaty of Versailles signed and which two sides did it end the war between? (4 marks)

4. 2.0 g of NaOH (M = 40 g/mol) reacts with excess HCl. Calculate the moles of NaOH, identify the limiting reagent, and give the theoretical yield of NaCl in grams. (5 marks)

5. Write a structured response arguing whether homework improves exam performance. Include a claim, evidence, reasoning and a conclusion. (5 marks)

# Brag Plan: WeHelpTeachers

## What is this app?
WeHelpTeachers is an AI exam evaluation engine that takes teacher rubrics and messy handwritten student answer sheets, analyzes formulas and diagrams with multimodal vision OCR, and outputs transparent step-by-step marks with every deduction explained.

## The angle
The universal pain of teachers drowning in stacks of handwriting late at night. Instead of an opaque "AI black box score", WeHelpTeachers evaluates like a meticulous senior teacher: matching every step, checking handwriting and diagrams, applying penalty rules, and explaining why every single mark was awarded or deducted.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on dark canvas:
"Grading 100 handwritten exams at 2 AM?"
A subtle red stack counter ticks: `1 / 100`, `2 / 100`...
Flash to: "Never again."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **1-Click Triad Drop:** Drop Question Paper + Rubric Key + Handwritten Script.
- **Multimodal Vision OCR Scanner:** Luminous mint laser sweeps across real handwritten student math (`x² - 3x - 10 = 0`, discriminant steps, physics lens diagram) verifying step by step.
- **4 Penalty Checkpoints in action:** Live badges for Handwriting, Diagrams, Smudges, and Formatting.
- **Instant Scoreboard & Report:** Score counter rises to `88 / 100 · Grade A`, with clear deduction tags (`-2 Step skipped`, `+4 Exact formula match`).

## Outro / punchline
"Grades that explain themselves."
Logo / Brand lockup: WeHelpTeachers
CTA: `Start grading at WeHelpTeachers`

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Drop Question Paper, Answer Key, and Student Sheet into 3 dropzones.
2. Action: Multimodal Vision OCR processes handwritten math steps and diagrams with 4 penalty criteria.
3. Result: Interactive dual-pane review with 88/100 scorecard, step breakdown, and instant report download.
